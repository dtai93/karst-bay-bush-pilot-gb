Limestone Wings sends the red-and-cream Kestrel K-2, tail number KW-07, over an original tropical karst bay of grey limestone pinnacles, jungle crowns, and turquoise water. Five sorties ask for different flying: tower rings, a melting ice cargo run, rooftop mail drops, a rainy crosswind landing, and a flare-boat rescue. Fuel burn, damage, and the landing grade turn each flight into a decision, and mission pay buys a bigger tank, a stronger engine, or tougher gear.

## Controls

Press 1–5 to choose a mission and Enter to launch. W/S or Up/Down pitch, A/D or Left/Right roll, Q/E rudder, Shift/Ctrl or the mouse wheel for throttle, F flaps, G brakes, C toggles chase and cockpit, mouse drag looks around, Space drops mail, H opens the hangar, P pauses, M mutes, and R restarts.

## Missions

1. First Light — fly six glowing rings around the towers and land at Harbour Field.
2. Ice for the Market — carry fish ice to Terrace Strip before it melts; the weight changes the wing.
3. Mail Drop — drop mail sacks onto the stilt-village roofs and score the accuracy.
4. Squall Line — land on Lagoon Strip in rain and crosswind with the tanks running low.
5. Lighthouse Rescue — find the flare boat, circle it to mark the crew, and return before the fuel runs out.

## What's new in v2

- Sculpted karst bay: faceted limestone pinnacles, cave mouths, jungle caps, and colour-banded water.
- A built Kestrel K-2: tapered fuselage, struts, tundra tyres, livery, and a visible propeller disc.
- Glass avionics: slim edge tapes for HDG, KTS, and ALT, plus a lit six-pack in the 3D cockpit.
- Sound and particles: engine, wind, stall horn, spray, rain, and touchdown dust.
- Four in-flight screenshot scenes: a ring pass, short final, a mail drop, and a graded touchdown.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Alternatively, run `python -m http.server -d game 8080` and open http://localhost:8080.
